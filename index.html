L

Locate the root

It's never really about lust. It's about the gap you're trying to fill.

What keeps bringing you back

You know your purpose and you're on it. For a long time that alone silenced the demon. Now it comes back every so often, louder, because it knows more about you.

You're living in your dream city, in your dream job, and the relationship is going well. Strangely, that's the danger. The closer you get to your purpose, the more the uncertainty scares you.

Your root

Uncertainty. The enemy plants doubt about your dreams, and you fill that uncomfortable space with porn, with alcohol, with going out. You do it because you're afraid to go deeper.

When you've escaped it before, you loved more than you ever had and saw things a lustful man never gets access to. That's what's on the other side.

Ask yourself when it hits

What am I uncertain about right now? What am I avoiding? Name that, not the urge.

I

Interrupt the pattern

The urge will come. It's a forever war. Break the chain before it breaks you.

You are not your urges

Most men live in fusion: I have an urge, so I act on it, like a dog. Acceptance and Commitment Therapy says you're separate from your urges. They're just there. Not good, not bad. An urge rises, peaks and falls away.

The boulder

Picture a boulder at the top of a peak with you at the bottom. However long you let the urge sit is how hard the boulder gets pushed. Once it gets a nudge it picks up speed, and there's no outrunning it. You get crushed.

"One more scroll, one more video" is a lie. It's 1, 2, 3, go, or you're falling.

Your specific triggers

Time: late at night, lights off, before bed.

Place: the bedroom or bathroom, or watching football alone at night.

Emotion: alone, or hungover with self control out the window.

Behaviour: scrolling Instagram, then Twitter, then something shows too much, then a video, then it's done. You scroll because you want the algorithm to show you this.

Physical state: tired, weary, or scared of approaching something. And 100% of the time, social media is involved.

Your response has to be

Physical: stand up, leave the room, turn the phone off, walk, call someone, do push-ups, read the Bible away from the device.

Immediate: because of the boulder. No delay.

Distancing: get the device away from you. If you're lonely, call someone, but the phone goes.

If-then protocols

Night: phone away while watching football at home, or don't watch it.

Alcohol: hungover is when you fall most. Don't get drunk.

Scroll: block Twitter, remove the account. It will never work for you.

If you fall: tell your brothers and work back through this framework for a full 7-day cycle.

G

Guard the gates

The highest return of anything here. Nail this and 90 to 95% of it goes.

You're a fort

Strong, fortified. But drop the drawbridge in one area, usually by getting drunk, and the enemy walks in and wrecks the place. It's rarely lust that starts it. It's a crack in the wall you never mended.

A man without self control is like a city broken into and left without walls.Proverbs 25:28

Your gates

Mindless scrolling on Instagram and Twitter. Being hungover, sometimes for days while your willpower rebuilds. Gluttony and overconsumption, whether food or content. Being lonely with free time and nothing to do. The same app, the same room, the same time.

Cut it out before the urge hits

Phone out of the bedroom, or at least out of reach. Delete the app. Install a blocker like Opal or Covenant Eyes. No screens after a set time. Clean up and monitor your feeds. Fast when overconsumption creeps in.

If your right eye causes you to sin, tear it out and throw it away. It is better that you lose one of your members than that your whole body be thrown into hell.Matthew 5:29

Good intentions are not a good enough guard. Don't talk about your hopes. Talk about your decisions and your actions. And don't be too proud: you don't have the willpower to fight this head on.

Keep pictures of the people you love, and reminders, in your spaces.

H

Hide nothing

Secrecy is the enemy's greatest weapon. Freedom starts in the light.

Go to the medic

A soldier wounded in battle who won't go to the medic gets slower and eventually bleeds out. He could've just gone. Lust festers in the dark, alone, in shame and pride.

Condemnation versus conviction

CondemnationAttacks your identity. Shame, isolate, self sabotage, feel unworthy, repeat. It leaves you drained and believing you are the sin.
ConvictionAttacks the sin, not you. You grieve how evil it is, then bring it to God. Your identity is in Christ, not in the habit.

The cycle that breaks it: sorrow, then repentance, then confession to God and your brothers, then mercy, then grace.

Whoever conceals his transgressions will not prosper, but he who confesses and forsakes them will obtain mercy.Proverbs 28:13

Confession needs three things

Speed: immediately. Specifics: describe it, even when it's uncomfortable. Support: men who'll tell you what this does to you and your future marriage, not men who say "it's okay."

Cross carriers

Brothers who share a vision and talk about deep things. They help you carry the cross. You need them.

T

Train the replacement

The life changer. Pull the weed and plant something in its place.

Get the roots

Most men cut off the leaves and the branch while the roots stay deep. Whatever you pull out has to be replaced.

Do I have what it takes?

Can I handle the pressure? Can I protect the people I love? Can I be trusted? When you fall, at some point you've decided the answer is no. You feel the wound, things get hard, you feel overlooked or rejected or uncertain, and you reach for the quick fix.

What lust promisesConquest, reward, power. A fake sense of manhood.
What it gives youShame, no change and a deeper wound. And it's never enough.

Stop answering with a counterfeit

Build agency. Take dominion and rule over your world and your tribe. Build a life you don't need to escape from. And surrender control: alone you don't have what it takes for your salvation.

Flee youthful passions and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, along with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart.2 Timothy 2:22
FleeLust, laziness, lack of discipline
PursueRighteousness, purpose, impact

Replace pleasure with purpose

If you fall, you're saying pleasure outweighs purpose in your life. Once you're moving, momentum is your best friend. Gym. Running. Waking when you say. Sleeping when you say. Time passes either way; some men compound and others stay the same. Pick which one you are.
